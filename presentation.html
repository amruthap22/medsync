Identity & Privacy Protocol

Unique Patient ID Pass (`MED-P-XXXXXXXX`)

Cryptographically provisioned identity without exposing health records.

🔑 Automated Provisioning Workflow

  • UUID-Generated Format: Generated automatically during patient registration as MED-P-XXXXXXXX.
  • Collision Prevention: Enforced by unique database indices preventing duplicate or guessed IDs.
  • Persistent Digital Pass: Stored permanently in patient profile; survives session logouts and reboots.
  • Digital Pass Actions: Offers 1-click Copy ID, Download ID Card File, and Share with Doctor.

🛡️ Zero-Knowledge Security Principle

  • Identification Only: Possessing a Patient ID grants ZERO direct medical record access.
  • Explicit Handshake Required: Attending doctors must query the ID and submit a formal access request.
  • Patient Sovereignty: Patient must explicitly approve consent on their dashboard before data unbinds.
  • Audit Logging: Every query, approved grant, and revoked permission is immutably logged with timestamps.

Security & Compliance

Two-Party Consent & Audit Architecture

Strict compliance-focused protocol ensuring zero unauthorized record exposure.

Step 01

ID Exchange

Patient shares Patient ID (MED-P-7K92X) with attending physician during clinic visit or hospital registration.

Step 02

Access Request

Doctor queries ID in Doctor Hub. Backend generates an explicit access request with doctor details and timestamp.

Step 03

Patient Consent

Patient dashboard displays real-time consent modal. Patient clicks Approve or Reject at their discretion.

Step 04

Audit Trail

Upon approval, records unlock for doctor. Action is permanently logged in the immutable audit table.

Data Architecture

Relational Database Modeling & Entities

Normalized relational schema ensuring transactional integrity and zero data redundancy.

📑 Core Identity & Clinical Tables

  • users: uuid (PK), email, password_hash, full_name, role.
  • patient_profiles: patient_id (Unique PK), blood_group, city, emergency_contact.
  • medications: id, patient_name, medicine_name, dosage, frequency, doctor_name.
  • reminders: id, medication_name, time, note, status (Taken/Upcoming).

🔐 Governance & Coordination Tables

  • doctor_patient_access: id, doctor_name, patient_id, status (Approved/Pending), approved_at.
  • audit_logs: id, doctor_name, patient_id, action, timestamp, ip_address.
  • appointments: id, doctor_name, specialty, appointment_date, time, status.
  • messages: id, sender, recipient, message, timestamp, is_read.
